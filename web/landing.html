How it works

  1. 1

    The family shares

    Children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren send photos and videos from their phones, with a few words if they like.

  2. 2

    It shows up on Grandma’s frame

    A touchscreen at her house shows the newest photos, with a big card for each one that says who it’s from.

  3. 3

    She answers with a tap or her voice

    A heart or a ready-made note like “Call me” goes to whoever sent the photo, and a voice message goes to the whole family. Their phones let them know straight away, and it all shows in Grandma’s Feed.

What the family sees

Her answers, right on your phone

An app on your phone’s home screen, with no password to remember. Three tabs:

  • Family. Everyone’s photos and videos, newest first.
  • Grandma’s Feed. Every heart, note and voice message she sends, with the photo it was about.
  • Mine. What you shared, with her reactions on each.

When she answers your photo, your phone lets you know.

Made to be easy on Grandma

  • Nothing to manage

    No settings, no menus and no logins on her screen. Someone in the family looks after it from afar.

  • One tap from home

    A big Home button is always in the same corner, on every screen.

  • Nothing plays by itself

    Videos and voices play only when she taps them.

  • Big and calm

    Large print, big buttons and few choices: four reactions, not forty. At night the screen dims and rests.

  • It just keeps working

    It starts again on its own after a power cut or a Wi-Fi drop, and keeps showing saved photos in the meantime.

  • Private by default

    Only invited family can see anything or send anything.

Private, the whole way through

  • Invite only. Nobody joins without an invite from the family.
  • No ads, no tracking. Nothing here counts you, follows you or sells to you.
  • Nothing public. No profiles, no search and no share links.
  • Photos stay with the family. They’re kept on the family’s own server, not a photo service.

Invite only. Ask the person who runs your family’s frame for an invite.
